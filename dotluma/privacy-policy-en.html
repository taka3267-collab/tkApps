Privacy Policy

DotLuma - LED Banner

日本語

Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. Introduction

T.K Apps ("we") respects the privacy of people who use the iOS app DotLuma - LED Banner ("the App"). This policy explains how the App handles information.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information through the App.

The App has no account registration, and we do not run any server for it. The text and settings you enter are never sent to us.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following information only on your device.

TypeDetails
Messages and display settingsThe text you enter, plus your colors, style, font, size, speed, effects and most recent settings
PresetsSettings you save under a name
Purchase statusWhether DotLuma Pro has been purchased, kept for display purposes
Export filesTemporary files created while making a video or GIF

If you use your operating system's backup features, this information may be included in those backups. You are responsible for managing them.

4. Photo Library Access

The App may ask for permission to add to your photo library so it can save videos and GIFs you export. It asks only when you choose "Save to Photos".

This permission is used only to add items. The App never reads the photos in your library.

5. Sharing

If you share an exported video, GIF, DotLuma file or share link, the terms and policies of the service you share it with apply.

DotLuma files and share links contain your text and display settings. In a share link, this data is placed after the "#" in the URL, so it is not sent to the server when the page opens and is handled only in the browser. Anyone who receives the link can read it.

6. Ads, Analytics and Tracking

The App does not use ads, analytics tools or tracking.

If this changes, we will update this policy.

7. In-App Purchases

The in-app purchase (DotLuma Pro) is processed through Apple's StoreKit and the App Store. We never receive or store payment information such as credit card numbers.

  • Apple Privacy Policy

8. Sharing with Third Parties

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not provide it to third parties.

9. Deleting Your Data

You can delete presets in the App at any time. If you uninstall the App, the data it stored on your device is deleted according to your operating system's behavior.

Videos and GIFs saved to your photo library or shared elsewhere are yours to manage and delete.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when needed. Any changes will be published on this page with a new update date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us.
