2. What the App does

The App adds, changes, and removes the location (latitude and longitude) of photos and videos in the Photos app on your device.

3. Changing your photos

  • Saving or removing a location changes the photo itself in the Photos app. With iCloud Photos, the change also appears on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account.
  • Please check the photos and place on the confirmation screen before saving.
  • You can reverse the latest change with Undo on the result screen, but this may not be possible if the photos were deleted or changed afterwards.
  • We recommend backing up important photos beforehand.
