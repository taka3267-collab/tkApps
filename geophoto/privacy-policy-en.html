Privacy Policy

GeoPhoto - GPS Editor

日本語

Last updated: November 8, 2026

1. Introduction

T.K Apps ("we") respects your privacy. This policy explains how the iOS app "GeoPhoto - GPS Editor" (the "App") handles information.

The App never sends your photos or locations to us. We run no servers for the App, and it has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.

2. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following only on your device.

TypeContents and purpose
Recent placesName, address, and coordinates of places you saved to photos (up to 8), so you can pick them again quickly.
Edit historyIdentifiers of photos you changed and their previous coordinates (last 30 edits), used for Undo.

The App never copies or stores the photos themselves (image data).

3. Photos and the photo library

The App asks for photo library access so it can add, change, or remove the location of your photos.

  • To show your photos, it reads thumbnails, capture dates, and whether each photo has a location.
  • It changes the location (latitude and longitude) of the selected photos only when you tap Save Location, Remove Location, or Undo. Image content, capture dates, and albums are not changed.
  • iOS may ask you to confirm before photos are changed.
  • The App does not analyze what is in your photos.
  • If you use iCloud Photos, changed locations sync to your other devices through Apple's iCloud.
  • You can change photo access at any time in the Settings app.

4. Current location

The App gets your current location only when you tap the Current Location button on the place picker, and uses it to drop a pin on the map. It never tracks your location continuously or in the background.

Without location access, you can still choose places by tapping the map or searching.

5. Maps and place search

The App uses Apple MapKit to show maps, search for places, and look up place names from coordinates (reverse geocoding). For this, your search text, the visible map area, and the coordinates you choose are sent to Apple and handled under Apple's Privacy Policy.

  • Apple Privacy Policy

6. Ads, analytics, and tracking

The App shows no ads, collects no analytics or crash data, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

7. Sharing with third parties

We do not collect your information, so we do not share it. Apart from the Apple services described above (MapKit and iCloud Photos), the App sends no information off your device.

8. Deleting data

Deleting the App removes its recent places and edit history from your device. Locations you saved to photos stay with the photos; you can remove them with the App's Remove Location or in the Photos app.

9. Children's privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
