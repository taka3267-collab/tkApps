An easy place
to keep today.

Look at the photos you took that day, and write a line on the days you want to.

CONCEPT

Start from
a photo.

There are plenty of diary apps already. I built this one so that, before “what should I write,” there is “oh right — that's what that day was like.”

Keep it at your own pace.

You don’t have to write every day — keep it at whatever pace suits you. Open it when you feel like it and write as much as you want. Even a few days a month add up to a record of your year once you read them back.

Photos help you remember.

Your camera roll is the most honest record you keep. Lay those photos faintly over the calendar and, even after a while away, you can start from "ah, I ate that."

Open it on the days you want to.
That is all this diary asks.

Make writing
as short as possible.

Title, mood, score and tags are all optional. Write the body, close it, and it's an entry. Hide the fields you never use and the screen stays uncluttered. Up to 10 photos per entry, and you can open the camera right there.

Days with no entry
show that day’s photo.

On days with no entry yet, the calendar shows the photo you took that day, faintly behind the date. One familiar shot and the memory comes back on its own. In the editor you can pick straight from that day's photos too. You can turn it off in Settings.

42 colors,
one notebook of your own.

Bright solids, calm traditional Japanese tones, and gradients — 42 colors for the whole app. The Home Screen icon can follow the color you pick. Typeface, text size and line spacing are adjustable too, so it reads the way you like.

See how the
weeks actually went.

Give the day a score from 0 to 100 and look back over one week, one month or two. Filter by tag and patterns surface — "it always dips after this." Don't want it? Hide the field entirely.

Everything else it does

The things a daily record needs are all here.

Widgets

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show the days written this month and your latest entry. A Control Center control starts a new one in a tap.

Siri & Shortcuts

Say "write a diary entry" to start one. It opens from Spotlight too.

Search & tags

Search across body text, titles and tags. Tag entries and read them back by theme.

iCloud sync

Syncs across devices on the same Apple Account, so a new phone picks up where you left off.

Backup

Export entries and photos to a single file and restore them later.

Export

Save as PDF or plain text. Print it if you want it on paper.

Reminders

A quiet notification at the time you choose. Write on a future date and it tells you on the day.

App lock

Lock the app on launch with Face ID or a passcode.

Your diary stays on your device.

No account, no sign-in. Entries are stored on your device — and, if you turn it on, in your own iCloud private database. Nothing is sent to a server of ours. Photo access is used only to show that day's photos as suggestions, and nothing is uploaded.

Premium (optional)

Writing, photos, tags, search and sync are all free. Premium removes the ads and lifts the export limit. Monthly, yearly or one-time — the yearly plan comes with a one-week free trial.

No ads

Banners and full-screen ads stop appearing.

Unlimited PDF export

The free version exports the 3 most recent entries; Premium exports them all.

Unlimited text export

For when you want to move your writing elsewhere.

Questions

How much can I do for free?
Writing, photos, tags, search, calendar, scores, iCloud sync and backup are all free. The only differences are the export limit (3 most recent entries as PDF) and the ads.
Where are my entries stored?
On your device. Turn on iCloud sync and they are also stored in your own iCloud private database and shared across your devices. The developer cannot read them.
What happens when I get a new phone?
With iCloud sync on, install the app and sign in — that's it. With it off, export a file from Settings > Backup & Transfer and import it on the new device.
Do I have to allow photo access?
No. Without it you can still add photos through the standard photo picker. Access is only needed to list the photos taken on that day; decline and that row simply doesn't appear.
How do I remove the ads?
Settings > Premium. Monthly, yearly and one-time options; the yearly plan has a one-week free trial.
Is there a streak counter?
No. A diary isn't something to compete at, so you can keep it at whatever pace suits you.
Widgets? Apple Watch?
Widgets work on both the Home Screen and the Lock Screen, and a Control Center control opens a new entry. There is no Apple Watch app for now.
