Privacy Policy

AnimeDeck

日本語

Last updated: October 19, 2026

1. Introduction

T.K Apps ("we") respects your privacy. This policy explains how the iOS app "AnimeDeck" (アニメデッキ, the "App") handles your information.

The App shows no ads and has no in-app purchases. We do not collect your personal information.

2. Information Stored by the App

The App stores the following information you enter or select, on your device only.

TypeDetails
Anime informationTitle, original title, format (TV, movie, etc.), studio, genre, air date, episode count, episode length, poster image URL, synopsis, and JAN code (when added from a Blu-ray/DVD), obtained from anime information services or entered by you
Watch logStatus (Plan to Watch, Watching, Completed, Dropped), episodes watched, how you watched it, rating, dates added/started/finished, reviews, notes, series name, tags, and tier lists
PhotosPhotos you register as posters, and images you capture or choose to scan text into notes
App settingsAppearance, theme color, shelf display settings, region (Japan / outside Japan), rating style, search history, etc.
Exported dataShelf data you export as a backup (JSON) or CSV

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your shelf, watch log, and photos are stored on your device. The App has no server of its own, and we never receive or store your watch log or photos. No account is required.

To show them in the Home Screen widget, the titles, poster images, and counts of the anime you are watching are saved to a shared area on the same device (App Group).

If you use iOS backups, sharing, or export features, you are responsible for managing the data at those destinations.

4. Anime Information Services

To search for anime and load anime information and posters, the App uses the following external services. When you search, your search terms (title or JAN code) and the display language (Japanese / English) are sent to these services. Your watch log and other personal information are never sent. Downloaded images are cached on your device for display.

  • Anime information and posters: the TMDB API, provided by TMDB (The Movie Database)
  • Blu-ray/DVD information (Japan region only): the Rakuten Books DVD/Blu-ray Search API, provided by Rakuten Group, Inc.

This application uses TMDB and the TMDB APIs but is not endorsed, certified, or otherwise approved by TMDB.

  • TMDB Privacy Policy
  • Rakuten Group Privacy Policy

5. External Links

The App contains links to external websites. When you open them, the linked services' own policies apply. The App's links do not use any affiliate (referral fee) programs.

  • Amazon and Rakuten Books pages for finding Blu-rays and DVDs
  • Google Search, opened from "Search the Web" (streaming, reviews)
  • TMDB pages, opened from "View on TMDB"

6. Camera, Photos, and Photo Library

The App may ask for camera access to scan the barcode (JAN code) on a Blu-ray or DVD. The camera feed is used only to read the barcode and is never saved or sent.

The App may also ask for camera or photo library access so you can add poster photos and scan text into notes. Text recognition uses on-device Live Text, and images are never sent off your device. Registered photos are stored only on your device.

7. Ads, Tracking, and Purchases

The App does not show ads. It contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

The App has no in-app purchases.

8. Sharing with Third Parties

Because we do not collect your information, we do not share it with third parties. As described in Section 4, search terms are sent to external services to perform searches.

9. Deleting Your Data

You can delete anime, watch logs, photos, notes, tags, series, tier lists, and search history within the App. When you uninstall the App, its data on your device is deleted according to iOS behavior.

Please manage and delete any backups or CSV files you exported yourself.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as needed. Updates will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
