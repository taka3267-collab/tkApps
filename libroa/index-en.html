BookDeck

A reading log you build up and enjoy looking at

Line up the books you've read and want to read, by their covers.

BookDeck is an iOS app for searching books, adding them to your own shelf, and recording their status, ratings, memos and tags. It isn't a social network — it's there to keep your own reading log, simply and comfortably. There is no account to create, and your data is stored on your device and in your own iCloud.

iOS Reading log Bookshelf Barcode

A shelf where covers come first

Books you add line up as a grid of covers. Filter by Want to read, To read, Reading or Finished, or narrow things down with tags and search.

Add by search or barcode

Search by title, author or ISBN, or scan the barcode on the back of a book. You can also add several books at once.

Organize with series and tags

Manga and light novels can appear as one stack per series. Tags are yours to create, rename and tidy up later.

Your records stay with you

Record a status, a five-star rating, start and finish dates, and memos for each book. If the cover doesn't match your copy, swap in a photo of your own.

Premium

The shelf, search, tags and the other basics are all free. Premium hides the ads and unlocks CSV export of your shelf.

  • No ads at all
  • CSV export of your shelf (opens in Excel or Numbers)
  • Monthly, yearly or a one-time purchase (the yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial)
